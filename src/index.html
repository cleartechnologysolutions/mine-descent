Recover one new cannon model in each mine. Special cannons use their own found ammunition. Pulse fire is unlimited. Empty cannons switch to Pulse when you try to fire. After a sustained burst, tap Space for single shots during a brief cooling window; rapid fire then returns.

Shields absorb damage first and never regenerate. Collect shield cells to restore them. Destroyed enemies often drop glowing blue cells; drops are more likely when shields are low. Red screen edges warn when shields reach 25% or less. Hull needs repair pickups. Hull at zero destroys your ship.
Unlimited retries. Restart this sector repaired and rearmed; progress earned within it resets.
